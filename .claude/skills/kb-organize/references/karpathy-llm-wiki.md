# LLM Wiki Method Summary

The LLM Wiki approach treats knowledge management as continuous bookkeeping.

Core idea:

- Humans decide what is worth remembering.
- AI maintains structure: indexes, backlinks, summaries, tags, and concept pages.
- Raw notes become more valuable when they are connected to durable concepts.

Recommended layers:

1. Raw material and source notes.
2. Concept pages that synthesize current understanding.
3. Indexes that route humans and AI to the right pages.
4. Rules that keep future maintenance consistent.

Practical implications:

- Do not wait for a perfect taxonomy before writing.
- Keep concept pages alive with append-only timelines.
- Prefer small, repeated maintenance passes over large reorganizations.
- Preserve source boundaries so original judgment and external references do not blur.
